Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026
Website: https://mariusmarai.com

1. Data Controller & Contact

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is handled on mariusmarai.com (operated by Marius Marai, based in Bucharest, Romania)[cite: 1].

For any privacy questions, data requests, or notices, please contact: hello@mariusmarai.com.

2. Newsletter & Subscription Data (MailerLite)

If you choose to subscribe to the newsletter or updates published on this website:

  • Data collected: Your email address (and any optional fields you explicitly provide in the subscription form).
  • Consent & Double Opt-In: Subscriptions use a double opt-in workflow. Your email address is added to the active mailing list only after you confirm your subscription via the verification message sent to your inbox.
  • Purpose & Legal Basis: We process your email address solely to deliver requested articles, updates, or announcements, based on your explicit consent (for example, under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR where applicable).
  • Processor: Subscriber management and email delivery are performed by MailerLite (MailerLite Limited / UAB MailerLite), acting as a data processor under applicable data protection terms.
  • Retention & Unsubscribe: Your email address is kept until you unsubscribe or request deletion. Every email contains an Unsubscribe link, and you may also request access, correction, or deletion at any time by emailing hello@mariusmarai.com.

3. Website Analytics (GoatCounter)

This website uses GoatCounter for privacy-conscious traffic measurement (such as page views, referrers, and general browser/device statistics)[cite: 1].

  • GoatCounter is configured without advertising trackers or cross-site behavioral profiling cookies.
  • Aggregated statistics help understand which content is most useful to readers while respecting visitor privacy.

4. Hosting, DNS & Security Infrastructure (GitHub Pages & Cloudflare)

  • Hosting: Static website files are delivered via GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.)[cite: 1].
  • DNS, Routing & Security: Domain resolution, HTTPS encryption, security controls, and Cloudflare Email Routing (used to route emails sent to hello@mariusmarai.com) are provided by Cloudflare, Inc.[cite: 1].
  • As part of standard internet delivery, routing, and security protection, Cloudflare and GitHub may process technical connection metadata (such as IP addresses, request headers, and timestamps) in accordance with their respective security and privacy practices.

5. Cookies & Privacy Signals

This website does not deploy advertising or third-party tracking cookies. Only technical mechanisms strictly necessary to serve pages, route requests, or handle user-initiated actions (such as language selection or form submission) are used. Where browser-based privacy signals such as Global Privacy Control (GPC) are supported and applicable, reasonable efforts are made to honor non-tracking preferences.

6. International Data Transfers & Global Privacy Rights

Because mariusmarai.com is accessible globally and uses cloud infrastructure and service providers (MailerLite, Cloudflare, GitHub) that may process data outside your country of residence, personal data may be transferred internationally subject to applicable legal safeguards and provider terms[cite: 1].

Depending on where you reside, you may have specific rights regarding your personal information:

  • EEA & United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR): Rights of access, rectification, erasure ("right to be forgotten"), restriction of processing, objection, data portability, withdrawal of consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Romania: ANSPDCP; in the UK: ICO).
  • United States (including California CCPA/CPRA and other US state privacy laws): Where applicable, rights to know/access categories and specific pieces of personal information, request deletion or correction, and opt out of "sale" or "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • LATAM, Canada, Asia-Pacific & Middle East (such as Brazil LGPD, Canada PIPEDA / Quebec Law 25, Singapore PDPA, Japan APPI, South Korea PIPA, China PIPL, UAE/Saudi PDPL, Australia/NZ privacy laws): Subject to applicable local laws, you may have rights to request information about processing and cross-border transfers, access, correct, or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent. If you are in a jurisdiction with specific cross-border or local disclosure requirements (such as China's PIPL or specific US states) and wish to request additional details or deletion, please contact hello@mariusmarai.com.

7. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect technical, operational, or legal developments. The "Last updated" date at the top indicates the current version.

Data Protection & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026
Website: https://mariusmarai.com

1. Data Controller and Contact

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is handled on mariusmarai.com (operated by Marius Marai, based in Bucharest, Romania).

For any privacy questions, data requests, or notices, please contact: hello@mariusmarai.com.

2. Newsletter and Subscription Data (MailerLite)

If you choose to subscribe to the newsletter or updates published on this website:

  • Consent-Gated Script Loading: The external MailerLite script (universal.js) is not loaded automatically by default; it is injected only after you choose Accept All in the consent banner or click the enable button inside the subscription block. If you switch back to Essentials Only, the script and any rendered form elements are removed (and re-enabling after prior initialization in the same session reloads the page cleanly).
  • Data collected: Your email address, any optional fields you explicitly provide in the subscription form, and standard technical subscription metadata automatically processed during sign-up (such as subscription timestamp, general region/time zone inferred from connection headers, and email engagement status) to ensure proper delivery and prevent spam.
  • Consent and Double Opt-In: Subscriptions use a double opt-in workflow. Your email address is added to the active mailing list only after you confirm your subscription via the verification message sent to your inbox.
  • Purpose and Legal Basis: We process your email address solely to deliver requested articles, updates, or announcements, based on your explicit consent (for example, under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR where applicable).
  • Processor: Subscriber management and email delivery are performed by MailerLite (MailerLite Limited / UAB MailerLite), acting as a data processor under applicable data protection terms.
  • Retention and Unsubscribe: Your email address is kept until you unsubscribe or request deletion. Every email contains an Unsubscribe link, and you may also request access, correction, or deletion at any time by emailing hello@mariusmarai.com.

3. Website Analytics and Search Visibility

This website uses Google Search, Bing Search, and GoatCounter Analytics for privacy-conscious traffic measurement and search performance monitoring (such as page views, referrers, search impressions, and general browser/device statistics).

  • GoatCounter Analytics: Configured without advertising trackers or cross-site behavioral profiling cookies. Aggregated statistics help understand which content is most useful to readers while respecting visitor privacy.
  • Google Search & Bing Search (Search Console / Webmaster Tools): Used strictly to monitor organic search indexing, crawl health, and aggregated, anonymized search query performance (such as search impressions, clicks, and general country/device trends) without placing tracking cookies on visitors or identifying individual users on this website. Any search activity performed directly on Google or Bing is governed by their respective privacy policies.

4. Hosting, DNS and Security Infrastructure (GitHub Pages and Cloudflare)

  • Hosting: Static website files are delivered via GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.).
  • DNS, Routing and Security: Domain resolution, HTTPS encryption, security controls, and Cloudflare Email Routing (used to route emails sent to hello@mariusmarai.com) are provided by Cloudflare, Inc.
  • As part of standard internet delivery, routing, and security protection, Cloudflare and GitHub may process technical connection metadata (such as IP addresses, request headers, and timestamps) in accordance with their respective security and privacy practices.

5. Cookies, Local Storage, Consent Withdrawal and Privacy Signals

This website does not deploy advertising or third-party tracking cookies. We use browser localStorage keys (mariusmarai_lang, mariusmarai_cookie_consent, and mariusmarai_cookie_consent_date) to remember your preferred language and consent choice, and load external services such as MailerLite only when full consent is granted.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time by clicking Cookie Settings in the footer of any page. Switching from Accept All to Essentials Only disables the MailerLite form fallback view and clears non-essential cookies set on the domain. Where browser-based privacy signals such as Global Privacy Control (GPC) are supported and applicable, reasonable efforts are made to honor non-tracking preferences.

6. International Data Transfers and Global Privacy Rights

Because mariusmarai.com is accessible globally and uses cloud infrastructure and service providers (MailerLite, Cloudflare, GitHub) that may process data outside your country of residence, personal data may be transferred internationally subject to applicable legal safeguards and provider terms.

Depending on where you reside, you may have specific rights regarding your personal information:

  • EEA & United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR): Rights of access, rectification, erasure ("right to be forgotten"), restriction of processing, objection, data portability, withdrawal of consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Romania: ANSPDCP; in the UK: ICO).
  • United States (including California CCPA/CPRA and other US state privacy laws): Where applicable, rights to know/access categories and specific pieces of personal information, request deletion or correction, and opt out of "sale" or "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • LATAM, Canada, Asia-Pacific & Middle East (such as Brazil LGPD, Canada PIPEDA / Quebec Law 25, Singapore PDPA, Japan APPI, South Korea PIPA, China PIPL, UAE/Saudi PDPL, Australia/NZ privacy laws): Subject to applicable local laws, you may have rights to request information about processing and cross-border transfers, access, correct, or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent. If you are in a jurisdiction with specific cross-border or local disclosure requirements (such as China's PIPL or specific US states) and wish to request additional details or deletion, please contact hello@mariusmarai.com.

7. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect technical, operational, or legal developments. The "Last updated" date at the top indicates the current version.
